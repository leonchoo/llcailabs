Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LLCAI Labs ("we", "our") handles information when you use the Number Merge mobile application ("the App"). By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Overview

The App is a local puzzle game. The core game experience runs entirely on your device. We do not operate user accounts, servers, or analytics systems, and we do not sell or rent any of your information.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To save your progress and preferences, the App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your highest score, current level and in-game progress
  • Game settings, such as board size and sound preference

This data never leaves your device. It is cleared when you reset app data or uninstall the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. When ads are served, Google may collect and process certain information, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and network information (for example, device model, operating system version, IP address, and network state)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about your interactions with advertisements

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, which may include personalized advertising. This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

Opting out of personalized ads: On Android, open Settings → Privacy → Ads and choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization". You may continue to see non-personalized ads.

4. How We Use Information

We ourselves do not collect or use any personal information. The only third party that processes data in connection with the App is Google AdMob, as described in Section 3.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any party. The information described in Section 3 is processed by Google in accordance with its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob and Google ads policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

6. Data Retention

Local data described in Section 2 remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is served in accordance with Google's policies for child-directed apps.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Stop data collection: You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising: As described in Section 3.
  • Access and questions: You may contact us at any time with questions about this policy or your data.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. Local data is stored in secure application storage on your device, and advertising data transmissions are encrypted by Google.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

llcailabs@gmail.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 14 日

本隐私政策说明 LLCAI Labs（"我们"）在您使用 Number Merge 移动应用（"本应用"）时如何处理信息。安装或使用本应用即表示您同意本政策所述做法。

1. 概述

本应用是一款本地运行的益智游戏，核心游戏体验完全在您的设备上完成。我们不运营用户账号、服务器或分析系统，也不会出售或出租您的任何信息。

2. 本地存储于设备的信息

为保存您的进度与偏好，本应用仅在设备本地存储以下数据：

  • 您的最高分、当前关卡与游戏进度
  • 游戏设置，如棋盘大小、音效偏好

这些数据不会离开您的设备；在您清除应用数据或卸载应用时一并删除。

3. 广告（Google AdMob）

本应用展示由 Google AdMob（Google LLC 提供的服务）提供的横幅广告。展示广告时，Google 可能收集并处理以下信息：

  • 广告标识符（如 Android 广告 ID）
  • 设备与网络信息（例如设备型号、操作系统版本、IP 地址、网络状态）
  • 由 IP 地址推断的近似位置
  • 您与广告的交互信息

Google 使用这些信息投放和衡量广告，其中可能包含个性化广告。该处理行为受 Google 自身隐私政策约束。

退出个性化广告：在 Android 设备上，进入 设置 → 隐私 → 广告，选择"删除广告 ID"或"退出广告个性化"。您仍可能看到非个性化广告。

4. 我们如何使用信息

我们自身不收集或使用任何个人信息。与本应用相关的唯一第三方数据处理方为 Google AdMob（见第 3 节）。

5. 信息共享

我们不会向任何第三方共享、出售或出租您的个人信息。第 3 节所述信息由 Google 依据其自身政策处理：

  • Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob 与 Google 广告政策：https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

6. 数据保留

第 2 节所述本地数据在您清除应用数据或卸载应用前保留在设备上。Google AdMob 处理的数据按其保留政策执行。

7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下（或您所在国家适用的最低年龄）儿童。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。广告投放遵循 Google 面向儿童应用的适用政策。

8. 您的权利与选择

  • 停止数据收集：卸载应用即可停止所有数据收集。
  • 退出个性化广告：操作方式见第 3 节。
  • 咨询与查询：您可随时就本政策或您的数据联系我们。

9. 数据安全

我们采取合理措施保护信息。本地数据存储于设备的安全应用存储区，广告数据传输由 Google 加密。

10. 政策更新

我们可能不时更新本隐私政策，变更将反映在本页面并更新"最后更新"日期。
