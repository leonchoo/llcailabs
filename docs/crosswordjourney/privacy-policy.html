Privacy Policy

Crossword Journey (com.llcailabs.crosswordjourney) — Effective date: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LLCAI Labs ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Crossword Journey (the "App").

1. Information we collect

The App is designed to be playable offline. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or precise location.

The following information may be collected automatically by third-party services when you use the App:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID) and device state, collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Crash and performance data (via Google Play services / Firebase, if enabled) to help us fix bugs.
  • In-app purchase and reward status is handled by Google Play and AdMob; we do not store payment details on our servers.

Your game progress (stars, coins, unlocked destinations, daily streak) is stored locally on your device only. If you uninstall the App, that data is deleted.

2. How we use information

We use the limited information above to:

  • Show you banner and rewarded ads (rewarded ads are optional — you choose to watch them to earn hints).
  • Diagnose crashes and improve gameplay.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles outside of Google's standard AdMob functionality.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to serve ads (including personalised ads) and to measure ad performance. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. See Google's advertising policy for details.

4. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age required by local law (13 in the US, 16 in the UK/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data retention

Locally stored progress data remains on your device until you clear the App data or uninstall. Ad-related and crash logs are retained by Google under its own policies.

6. Your choices

  • You can clear the App's storage at any time to erase local progress.
  • You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; declining an ad only means you do not earn the free hint.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Effective date" above.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: llcailabs@gmail.com.
