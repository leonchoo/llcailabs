What to include in your message

To help us reply quickly, please include:

  • App name and version (Settings → About inside the app).
  • Device model and Android version (Settings → About phone).
  • A short description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings if relevant.

Privacy requests

If your request involves personal data, please use the dedicated privacy channel and include enough information for us to verify your identity (the email address you originally contacted us from is usually sufficient).

See the Privacy Policy for the full data-handling statement and each app’s own privacy policy for product-specific details.
