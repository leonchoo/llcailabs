Contact

Three routes, depending on what you need. Product questions are usually answered faster by the app's own page than by email.

Start here

  • 1
    A question about an app or game

    Every product page carries an FAQ and its package name. Most questions about features, storage and permissions are answered there. Open support

  • 2
    Privacy or data deletion

    The privacy policy for the app you use names the address to write to, and the deletion page explains what to send. Privacy policies · Data deletion

  • 3
    Business, press or partnerships

    Write to the support address with “Business” in the subject line. A dedicated business mailbox is not confirmed yet, so none is published here. Our verified public identity is the Google Play developer page.

Email us

  • Supportllcailabs@gmail.com
  • Privacy & data deletionllcailabs@gmail.com
  • Business, press & partnershipsSame address, subject starting with “Business” — no separate mailbox is published.
One inbox, three subjects. Support and privacy requests both reach llcailabs@gmail.com, which is the same address named in the app privacy policies. Put the prefix in the subject line so the message can be routed.

What to include in your message

This is what lets us answer without a round trip.

  • App name and version — Settings → About inside the app.
  • Device model and Android version — Settings → About phone.
  • A short description and the steps to reproduce the problem.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if the issue is visual.

Privacy requests

If your request involves personal data, include enough detail for us to identify the record — the address you originally wrote from is usually sufficient.
