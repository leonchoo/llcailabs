🧠 Brain Twist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28 · Package: com.llcailabs.braintwist

English · 简体中文

1. What Brain Twist is

Brain Twist is an offline brain-teaser / puzzle game for Android. It requires no account and no login.

2. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect or transmit any personal data. The app does not ask for, and does not access:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your contacts / address book
  • Photos / media / files on your device
  • Microphone or camera
  • Precise or approximate location

All game progress — levels completed, stars, coins, hints, achievements, daily challenge state, language and theme settings — is stored only on your device (Android DataStore). It is never uploaded to our servers, because the app has no backend.

3. Advertising

The free version of Brain Twist uses Google AdMob to show:

  • Banner ads on Home / Level Map / Settings
  • Interstitial ads, shown occasionally after completing levels
  • Rewarded video ads, which you choose to watch for a free hint or extra coins

AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads. This is handled by Google under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or disable the advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

4. Children

Brain Twist is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Security and offline use

The game is fully playable offline. Advertising requires a network connection; if you are offline, ads simply do not load and the game continues normally. Your local progress is preserved across app restarts.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change the app's data practices, we will update this page and the in-app Privacy Policy screen. The date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

7. Contact

Questions? Contact: support@llcailabs.com

隐私政策（简体中文）

Brain Twist 是一款完全离线的脑筋急转弯游戏，不需要注册账号。

我们（开发者）不收集也不上传任何个人数据。应用不索取也不访问：姓名、邮箱、电话、联系人、相册/媒体文件、麦克风、相机或精确位置。所有游戏进度（关卡、星星、金币、提示、成就、每日挑战、语言与主题设置）仅保存在你的设备本地（Android DataStore），本应用没有后端，不会上传到任何服务器。

本应用面向一般用户，不针对 13 岁以下儿童。游戏可完全离线运行；离线时广告不加载，游戏正常进行。
