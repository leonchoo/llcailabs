Scan2Drive Data Deletion Request

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Scan2Drive respects your privacy and provides a way for users to request deletion of personal data associated with the app.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data associated with Scan2Drive, send an email to:

Email: llcailabs@gmail.com

Please include "Scan2Drive Data Deletion Request" in the subject line. You may also include the Google account email address associated with your Scan2Drive Google Drive connection so that we can identify the relevant authorization and data.

What Data Can Be Deleted

Depending on the request and the services you have used, we can assist with deletion of data associated with Scan2Drive, including:

  • Google Drive authorization information associated with Scan2Drive.
  • Scan2Drive-related authorization or connection information stored locally by the app.
  • Scan2Drive-generated PDF files stored locally on your device, when you delete them using the app or device storage controls.
  • Other personal information that Scan2Drive may have received and retained in connection with providing its services.

Google Drive Data

Scan2Drive uses Google OAuth to allow users to connect their Google Drive account. Scan2Drive does not create or manage Google accounts.

If you disconnect Google Drive from Scan2Drive or request deletion, Scan2Drive will remove the locally stored authorization information associated with the connection, where applicable.

Files that you have uploaded to your own Google Drive remain under your control. If you want those files permanently removed from Google Drive, you must delete them from your Google Drive account separately.

Data Stored on Your Device

Scan2Drive may create or store PDF files and related application data locally on your device.

Local files can be deleted directly by the user through the app's available file-management functions or by removing the application's data from the device.

Data We Do Not Retain

Scan2Drive does not operate its own user-account database and does not maintain a separate Scan2Drive account containing your password. Google account authentication is handled by Google through OAuth.

Scan2Drive does not intentionally retain your Google account password.

Data Retention

Scan2Drive does not intentionally retain personal data longer than necessary to provide its services.

Data stored locally on your device remains under your control until you delete it or remove the application's data. Files uploaded to Google Drive remain in your Google Drive until you delete them.

If you submit a data deletion request by email, information contained in the request may be retained only as reasonably necessary to process, verify, and document the request.

Processing Your Request

We may need to verify the request before deleting data in order to prevent unauthorized deletion of another user's information.

We will process valid deletion requests within a reasonable period after receiving and verifying the request.

Important: Deleting Scan2Drive data does not automatically delete files that you independently stored in your Google Drive account. Those files must be deleted from Google Drive by the account owner.
