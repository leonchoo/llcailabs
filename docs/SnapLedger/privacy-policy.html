Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

SnapLedger ("we", "the app") is an offline-first expense tracker that processes receipt images entirely on your device. This policy explains what data we collect and what we do not.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. SnapLedger does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not upload your receipts, expenses, or personal data to the internet.

  • Receipt images are stored only in your app's private storage.
  • Expense records (merchant, date, amount, category, payment method) are stored locally on your device using a local database.
  • OCR processing runs on-device via Google ML Kit. Images are not sent to any cloud OCR service.

AdMob

SnapLedger shows banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Data you control

You can export your data as CSV or a local backup ZIP at any time from Settings. You can delete the app to remove all data.

Children's privacy

SnapLedger is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data.
