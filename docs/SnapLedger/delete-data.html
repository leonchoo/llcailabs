SnapLedger — Data Deletion

Last updated: September 26, 2026

SnapLedger is an expense tracking app developed by LLCAI Labs.

SnapLedger is designed to work primarily on your device and does not require you to create an account.

How to Delete Your SnapLedger Data

Most SnapLedger expense data is stored locally on your device.

You can delete your locally stored SnapLedger data by using the deletion or data management features available in the app. You may also remove the app from your device to remove locally stored app data, subject to your device's backup settings.

Before deleting the app, please make sure you have exported or backed up any information you want to keep.

Receipt Images

Receipt images captured by SnapLedger are stored locally on your device.

You can delete individual receipt records or remove the app and its local data according to your device settings.

Account Data

SnapLedger does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Because there is no SnapLedger user account, LLCAI Labs does not maintain a SnapLedger account profile that users need to request deletion of.

Advertising Data

SnapLedger may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, to display advertisements.

Advertising providers may process certain information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings. If you want to manage or request deletion of data associated with a third-party advertising service, you may need to use the controls or privacy tools provided by that service.

Data Deletion Requests

If you have questions about your data or believe that LLCAI Labs may hold information associated with your use of SnapLedger that you would like deleted, you can contact us at:

Email: llcailabs@gmail.com

Please include "SnapLedger Data Deletion Request" in the subject line and provide enough information for us to understand your request.

We will review and respond to your request in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Important Information

Deleting SnapLedger from your device does not necessarily delete information that may have been independently collected or retained by third-party services, such as advertising providers. Such data is subject to the applicable third party's policies and deletion procedures.

For more information about how SnapLedger handles information, please refer to our Privacy Policy.
