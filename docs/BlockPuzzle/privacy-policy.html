Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

LLCaiLabs ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile application Block Puzzle (the "App"), available on Google Play. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared in connection with your use of the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself is a single-player offline puzzle game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from you directly. The App has no account system, no login, no chat, and no feature for uploading your data to our servers.

However, the App integrates third-party advertising services (Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK) in order to display banner advertisements. Through these services, third parties may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage, including:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID (e.g., Android Advertising ID / GAID);
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP);
  • Device type, operating system version, screen size, language settings;
  • App usage information and ad interactions (e.g., which ads were viewed or clicked).

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy policies. Please see Section 4 below for links.

2. Local Data

Your game progress (score, best score, sound settings) is stored locally on your device (in the app's private storage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to any third party. If you uninstall the App, this data is removed.

3. Advertising

The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. Advertising SDKs may use the advertising ID and other data to:

  • Deliver and measure advertisements;
  • Provide personalized advertising based on your interests (where legally permitted and subject to your consent choices).

You can control personalized advertising at any time:

  • On Android: open Settings → Privacy → Ads and enable "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID";
  • Use "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your Google account or device settings.

In regions where consent is required (e.g., the European Economic Area, the UK, and California), you may be asked to grant or deny consent to personalized ads through a consent management platform before personalized advertising is shown.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policy information.

We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy for details on how Google processes data collected through advertising SDKs.

5. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions required for its functionality:

  • INTERNET — used exclusively to load advertisements from Google's ad servers. No other network use occurs.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or other sensitive data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information. If the App is designated for families or if you are subject to COPPA obligations, personalized advertising is disabled and Google's certified families-friendly ad serving may apply.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access or correct your data;
  • The right to delete your data;
  • The right to object to or restrict processing, including opting out of personalized advertising;
  • The right to data portability (GDPR);
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights (CCPA/CPRA).

To exercise any of these rights, or to ask questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us using the details in Section 10. For data processed by Google's advertising services, you may also exercise rights directly through your Google account settings.

8. Data Retention and Security

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we do not retain any data on our servers. Data processed by Google's advertising SDKs is retained in accordance with Google's own retention and security policies. Communications with ad servers are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by updating this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
