KuizKu Privacy Policy

Application: KuizKu (package com.llcailabs.kuizku)
Developer: LLCAI Labs  ·  Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Last updated: 29 September 2026  ·  App version this policy applies to: 1.0.0

This policy explains what information KuizKu (“we / us / our”) handles when you use the KuizKu Android application, why it is needed, and the choices you have. KuizKu is an offline-first educational quiz application. It does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to register, and we do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Information processed while using the app falls into two groups:

TypeWhat it isWhere it is kept / sent
Learning data (stored only on your device) The education level/track you select, quiz and mock-exam answers, scores, history, and in-app scratch-pad notes. Local on-device database. It is not uploaded to us.
Device & advertising identifiers (collected by our advertising partner) Advertising ID and standard technical data used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads (see Section 4). Sent to Google for advertising purposes.

2. Permissions the app uses

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETDownload question-bank/content updates and load advertisements.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATECheck whether a network connection is available before syncing content or loading ads.
AD_ID (Advertising ID)Used by Google AdMob. On devices/settings where the advertising ID is reset or disabled, the advertising SDK behaves accordingly.
WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICEKeep background content updates running until complete.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, storage, or location permissions.

3. Content downloads & offline use

KuizKu downloads quiz question banks over the network so you can study offline. These requests fetch public content files; they do not include your answers or personal profile. After content is downloaded, normal studying, quizzes, mock exams and the scratch pad work without an internet connection.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

KuizKu displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. Google, as a third-party provider, may use cookies and device identifiers (including the Advertising ID) and collect data such as IP address, device model, operating system and interaction with ads in order to serve and measure advertising.

  • No full-screen/interstitial or rewarded ads are activated inside quizzes, mock exams, or result screens in the current version; banners appear only on allowed screens.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • How Google handles advertising data: see Google's Partner Sites policy and Privacy Policy.
Audience setting for publishers: KuizKu offers Primary and Secondary school content. We configure ad requests appropriately and do not knowingly request personalised advertising directed at children. Any school/“designed for families” declaration and child-directed treatment settings are managed in Google Play Console and AdMob in line with Google's Families policy.

5. How your information is used

  • To show the correct subjects, questions and progress for the level you choose.
  • To save your answers, results, history and scratch-pad work on the device.
  • To download updated quiz content.
  • To show and measure advertisements (via Google AdMob).
  • To keep the app working and secure (basic network/error handling).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your learning history for advertising profiling.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Information is shared only with the service provider required to run the app:

  • Google (AdMob / Google Play services) — advertising and related device identifiers, subject to Google's privacy policy linked above.
  • Public content hosting — the app fetches public question-bank files; no personal data is sent with those requests.

If the app is ever involved in a merger, acquisition or asset transfer, we will update this policy and continue to protect on-device data consistent with applicable law.

7. Data stored on your device & deletion

Your level selection, answers, scores, history and scratch-pad content are stored locally in the app's database. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling KuizKu removes this on-device learning data. Such data is not recoverable by us because we never upload it.

8. Security

Content updates are fetched over secure connections. Because personal learning data is kept only on your device rather than on our servers, there is no account for us to breach; please keep your device protected with a lock screen and current system updates.

9. Children

KuizKu provides educational content for Primary and Secondary school students and is suitable for family/school use. We do not knowingly ask children for personal information and do not create user accounts. On-device learning data stays on the device; advertising identifiers are handled through Google's applicable child-directed/family settings. Parents who wish to remove all local data may clear app data or uninstall the app.

10. Your choices & rights

  • Use the app without granting any account information — none is required.
  • Reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android system settings.
  • Delete on-device data via Android Settings → Apps → KuizKu → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling.
  • Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and applicable law, you may request access to, or correction/deletion of, any personal data a data controller holds about you; because we do not maintain accounts or server-side profiles, most data is local to your device.

11. Retention

We retain no account data because none is created. Google retains advertising data it collects under its own policies. On-device learning data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app.

12. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date. Material changes will also be reflected in an app update where appropriate.
