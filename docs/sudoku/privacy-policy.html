Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Sudoku (the "App") is a free puzzle game provided by llcailabs ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App and how it is handled. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any personal details, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information directly. All game progress (current puzzle, timer, mistakes, hints, settings) is stored only on your device in local storage and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage data to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID / AAID), IP address, and device type;
  • Information about your interactions with ads;
  • Approximate location, where permitted by your device settings.

This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and is governed by the AdMob program policies. You can learn how Google uses data from apps at How Google uses information from sites or apps.

3. Personalized Advertising & Opt-Out

AdMob may use the advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can control or reset this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID”.
  • You may also opt out of personalized advertising entirely (for example by enabling “Opt out of Ads Personalization” where available on your device).

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal information for money.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is a simple puzzle game intended for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising shown through AdMob is configured in accordance with Google's policies regarding apps directed to children where applicable. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

5. Data Retention & Security

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no personal data to retain or delete on our servers. Device-local game data is under your control and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date shown above.
