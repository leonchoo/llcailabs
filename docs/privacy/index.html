Privacy Policy

This page describes LLCAI Labs’ general privacy stance. Each app may collect different data; the per-app privacy policies below are authoritative.

1. Our privacy stance, in one line

If an app needs to know about you, we tell you. If it doesn’t, we don’t ask.

2. What we (generally) do

  • No account required. You can use every LLCAI Labs app without signing up.
  • Local-first data. Any data you enter is stored on your device unless an app’s privacy policy explicitly says otherwise.
  • No analytics SDKs. We do not embed Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any equivalent in our apps.
  • No fingerprinting. No device fingerprinting, no cross-app tracking.
  • AdMob is opt-in. In apps that include advertising, AdMob is disabled by default and serves non-personalized ads when you turn it on.
  • Cloud backup is opt-in. Where offered, cloud backup is manual and uses the smallest required scope (typically drive.file for Google Drive).

3. Per-app privacy policies

Each LLCAI Labs app has its own detailed privacy policy. If you have a question about a specific app, please refer to its policy first.

  • Age Calculator · com.llcailabs.agecalculator
  • Quick QR Scanner
  • Scan2Drive · com.llcailabs.scan2drive · Data Deletion Request
  • uConverter
  • Water Sort: Color Journey

4. What we (generally) don’t collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone input (unless an app’s policy explicitly states it).
  • Location, advertising IDs, persistent device identifiers.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Where AdMob is integrated, it is disabled by default. When you turn it on in an app’s Settings, it may serve non-personalized ads based on coarse device information. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device’s Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Children’s privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights & data deletion

Because all personal data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in system Settings. For apps that integrate Google Drive backup, you can also revoke Drive access from your Google account. A specific Data Deletion Request page is linked where applicable (see Scan2Drive above).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the bottom of this page.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or data deletion requests can be sent via the Contact page. We do not operate a customer support portal; the best way to reach us is email.
