Chrome Extension · Screenshot & Annotate

QuickShot

Screenshot. Annotate. Export.

A fast and privacy-friendly screenshot tool for Chrome.

Capture a region, visible page, or full webpage. Add annotations, blur sensitive information, and export your screenshot as PNG, JPG, or PDF.

Everything is processed locally on your device.

Get QuickShot Privacy Policy

Capture Any Webpage

Three capture modes, designed to be quick and predictable.

Region Capture

Select exactly the area you need with a simple drag.

Visible Capture

Capture the currently visible browser page instantly.

Full Page Capture

Capture long webpages with automatic scrolling and stitching.

Annotate in Seconds

Turn your screenshot into something useful immediately.

  • Pen. Draw freehand lines of any color and thickness.
  • Arrow. Point to the part that matters.
  • Rectangle. Outline a region with a sharp border.
  • Circle. Highlight an area with a round marker.
  • Text. Add a caption with adjustable size and color.
  • Number markers. Drop numbered steps in order for tutorials.
  • Blur. Smooth out pixels behind sensitive details.
  • Pixelate. Mosaic-style redaction for things you don't want to share.
  • Crop. Trim the canvas down to the part you actually need.

Protect Sensitive Information

Hide emails, phone numbers, account numbers, and other private information before sharing your screenshots. Blur and Pixelate are built into the editor and run entirely on your device.

Export Your Way

  • PNG. Lossless quality, the default for technical screenshots.
  • JPG. Smaller files with adjustable quality.
  • PDF. One-page document, ready to share or attach.
  • Copy to clipboard. Paste the finished image into chat, email, or another app.

Keep Your Screenshots Local

QuickShot is designed with privacy in mind.

  • No account required
  • No cloud upload
  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No tracking
  • No external server
  • No screenshot data sent to us

Every annotation, blur, and export happens inside your browser. The only file QuickShot writes outside of itself is the PNG, JPG, or PDF you choose to download.

Screenshot History

Keep your recent screenshots available locally for quick access. Recent captures are stored in your browser's local storage so you can revisit, re-annotate, or re-export them without re-capturing.

How It Works

Three steps from page to file:

  1. Capture. Region, visible page, or full page.
  2. Annotate. Pen, arrow, shapes, text, numbers, blur, pixelate, crop.
  3. Export. PNG, JPG, PDF, or copy to clipboard.

That is the entire workflow. No login, no upload, no “send to our cloud” step in the middle.
