About Sudoku - Classic Puzzle

Fill the 9×9 grid so every row, column and 3×3 box holds 1 to 9 exactly once. Straightforward, unhurried, and easy to pick up for a few minutes or a longer session.

What it does

  • Classic 9×9 Sudoku ruleset
  • Simple, uncluttered interface
  • Relaxing pace — play a puzzle at your own speed
  • Suitable for beginners as well as regular Sudoku players
  • No account required
