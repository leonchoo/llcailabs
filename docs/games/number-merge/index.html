About Number Merge

A calm number puzzle: combine matching tiles to build higher values, keep the board under control, and see how far you can get.

What it does

  • Merge matching numbers to create higher values
  • Simple rules that are quick to learn
  • Relaxing casual pace, good for short games
  • Clean, simple interface
  • Score chasing as the board grows

Questions

How do I play?

Combine matching numbers on the board; matching tiles merge into a higher value.

Is there a time limit?

No — you play at your own pace.
