About My Days

My Days keeps track of the dates that matter: your age and next birthday, the days you are counting down to, family birthdays, holiday calendars and date differences.

What it does

  • Exact age from your date of birth, in years and days
  • Next-birthday countdown and a saved birthday for quick access
  • Important days — birthdays, anniversaries, trips, exams, holidays and custom events — with countdowns
  • Family list with birthdays, ages, countdowns and notes
  • Calendar view with ICS calendar URL import and public holiday calendars
  • Date calculator for the difference between two dates

Questions

Is this the app formerly called Age Calculator?

Yes. It is listed on Google Play as My Days; the package name is unchanged (com.llcailabs.agecalculator), so existing installs update normally.

Does My Days show advertising?

The app's own privacy policy documents its advertising behaviour. Advertising is off by default and can be enabled in Settings; see the privacy policy for the current details.

Where are the dates I enter stored?

On your device. See the privacy policy for what is stored and how to remove it.
