About Age Calculator

Age Calculator is a simple, on-device utility for computing your exact age and the difference between two dates. Everything you type is stored only on your device, and AdMob advertising is disabled by default — turn it on only if you choose to.

Features

  • Exact age. Computes years, months, and days from a chosen birth date to today (or any reference date).
  • Living counter. Shows the number of days you have lived.
  • Date difference. Compute the gap between any two dates (years / months / days / total days).
  • Reminders. Optional local reminders for upcoming birthdays or anniversaries.

Privacy

Birth dates and reference dates you enter are stored locally on your device. AdMob advertising is off by default; if you turn it on in Settings it may serve non-personalized ads based on coarse device info. No account is required.
