Privacy Policy — MathNova

MathNova is an offline scientific calculator, formula reference and step-by-step solving assistant built for Malaysian secondary students (SPM Mathematics and Additional Mathematics). This page explains what the app does and does not collect.

App: MathNova  ·  Package: com.llcailabs.mathnova  ·  Developer: LLCAI Labs  ·  Contact: llcailabs@gmail.com  ·  Last updated: 2026-10-11

Short version

  • No account, sign-in or registration is required.
  • The calculator, formula library and solver work fully offline.
  • We do not run our own analytics, do not upload your calculations, and do not sell your personal data.
  • The only third-party data handling comes from Google AdMob, which serves a single low-interruption banner ad.

Data stored on your device only

MathNova stores the following locally on your device and never sends it to us:

  • Calculation history — the expressions and results you calculate, so you can view, copy or delete them in the app.
  • Preferences — language (English / Bahasa Melayu / 简体中文), light/dark theme, angle mode (DEG/RAD), display precision, and your favourited formulas.

You can clear calculation history any time in the app (Settings → Clear history). Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. There is no cloud account, so there is no server-side copy of your history or preferences.

Third party: Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of MathNova shows a single, low-interruption banner ad below the main content. No interstitial, rewarded, native or full-screen ads are shown. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google may process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID;
  • IP address (from which approximate, not precise, location may be derived);
  • Ad interaction and performance data used for ad serving and fraud prevention.

This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). If a banner fails to load, has no fill, or the device is offline, the ad space simply collapses to zero height — the calculator continues to work normally and no error is shown to you.

Children and target audience

MathNova is intended for secondary-school students and adults (SPM-level, generally age 13 and up). It is not primarily directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Because advertising is present, ad serving is configured consistent with Google Play's Families policy: no personalised advertising to users labelled as under the applicable age threshold.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect or upload your calculation inputs, equations or personal notes.
  • We do not request location, contacts, microphone, camera, storage or other sensitive permissions.
  • We do not share personal data with third parties other than the advertising service described above.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Permissions used

  • INTERNET — required only to load banner ads; the calculator itself works offline.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used by the ads SDK to decide whether an ad can be fetched.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — standard advertising identifier used by AdMob.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear your calculation history any time in Settings.
  • Reset or opt out of the Advertising ID via your device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

If you are located in Malaysia, you have rights under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) to access or request correction of your personal data. Because the app stores no personal data on our servers, most data lives on your device and is controlled by you.
