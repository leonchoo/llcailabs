About Quick QR Scanner

Quick QR Scanner is a fast, lightweight QR and barcode reader. Scan results, history, and settings live entirely on your device.

Features

  • Camera scanning. Point your camera at any QR or barcode (UPC, EAN, Code-128, etc.) and the value appears immediately.
  • History. Every scan is stored in a local SQLite (Room) database and available in the History screen for search, copy, share, or deletion.
  • Privacy switch. AdMob is opt-in — the app does not call AdMob by default. Turn it on only if you choose to.

Privacy

Scan results are stored only on your device. No account is required. AdMob (when enabled) is non-personalized by default. See the full privacy policy for details.
