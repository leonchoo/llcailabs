About uConverter

A small, reliable converter for the measurements you actually need, with a built-in BMI calculator. Calculations run on your device.

What it does

  • Length: metres, kilometres, centimetres, feet, inches and more
  • Weight: kilograms, grams, pounds and other common units
  • Area: square metres, square feet and more
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit and other scales
  • Data storage units and reference currency conversion
  • BMI calculator from height and weight; works offline for calculations

Questions

Are the currency rates live?

No. Currency conversion is a reference aid for information only and may not reflect live market rates.

Is the BMI result medical advice?

No. BMI is provided for general informational and wellness purposes only, not as medical advice.

Does it work offline?

Yes — the calculations run offline on your device.
