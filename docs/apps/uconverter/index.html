About uConverter

uConverter is a fast, accurate unit conversion tool. It does one thing, and it does it well — without collecting any personal data from you.

Features

  • Length (metres, feet, inches, miles, …)
  • Mass (kilograms, pounds, ounces, …)
  • Temperature (Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin)
  • Area, volume, time, speed and other common categories.

Privacy

The app does not collect any personal data. Specifically: no account is required, no personal information is collected, no usage analytics, no crash reporting SDK, no tracking pixel, and no fingerprinting.
