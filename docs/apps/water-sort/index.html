About Water Sort: Color Journey

A relaxing color-sorting puzzle. Tap a bottle to pour the top color into the next matching bottle, clear every bottle to win the level, then progress through the level-set.

Features

  • Hundreds of hand-designed levels. Play at your own pace.
  • No timer, no pressure. Pure puzzle feel.
  • Offline. No internet required.
  • Progress saved locally. Pick up where you left off.
