About SnapLedger

Photograph a receipt, let SnapLedger pick out the useful details, review them, and save the expense. Core tracking works offline and needs no account.

What it does

  • On-device text recognition pulls merchant, date, total, category and payment method from a receipt
  • You review and edit the extracted fields before anything is saved
  • Receipt images are stored locally on your device
  • Spending overview by total, recent expenses, category, merchant and month
  • Core expense tracking works offline
  • No account required

Questions

Does SnapLedger need an internet connection?

Core expense tracking is designed to work offline. Receipt images stay on your device.

Can I correct a receipt that was read wrong?

Yes. Extracted fields are shown for review and can be edited before you save the expense.

Use the deletion page linked below, or the in-app options described in the privacy policy.
