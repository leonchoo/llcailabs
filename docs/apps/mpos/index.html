About mPOS

mPOS is a free, offline-first point-of-sale app designed for small retail shops, market stalls, food trucks, and mobile sellers. All your point-of-sale data — products, orders, sales history, shop profile, and app settings — is stored locally on your Android device. mPOS does not require you to create an account to use it. Cloud backup to your personal Google Drive is entirely optional.

Features

  • Product and inventory management. Add products with name, price, category, SKU, stock quantity, and low-stock threshold. Edit prices, archive items, or mark them as unlimited-stock.
  • Fast checkout. Tap product cards to add items to the cart, adjust quantities, review subtotal and SST, then tap Bayar. Cash rounding to the nearest 5 sen is applied to the final total.
  • Sales history. Browse past orders by date. Tap any order to see the full item breakdown and amounts, and to reprint the receipt.
  • Receipt printing. Connect a Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal printer (58 mm or 80 mm) for paper receipts. Auto-print after checkout, or reprint manually from the order detail screen.
  • Stock and restock. See low-stock warnings on each product card and on the Reports dashboard. Restock quickly using the +5 / +10 / +20 chips or a custom quantity.
  • Backup and restore. Export a local backup file to your Downloads folder, or back up to your own Google Drive. Restore from any backup file to recover products, orders, and settings.
  • Optional Google Drive backup. Backup is opt-in and triggered manually by you. Sign in once with Google; mPOS uses the drive.file scope only and cannot touch the rest of your Google account.
  • Multi-language support. English, Bahasa Malaysia (ms), Bahasa Indonesia (id), and Simplified Chinese (zh-CN). The app follows your device language by default.

Privacy

mPOS does not require an account, does not collect analytics, and does not run any backend server. The only data that leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to share — AdMob advertising data and (optionally) Google Drive backup files.
