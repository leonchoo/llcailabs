About Scan2Drive

Scan2Drive turns your phone's camera into a portable scanner. Capture pages, save them as PDFs to your phone, and (optionally) sync to your personal Google Drive.

Features

  • Camera-based scanning. Auto-detect page edges, correct perspective, and save clean PDFs.
  • Local storage. PDFs are saved locally to Android/data/com.llcailabs.scan2drive/files/Documents/PDFs/. Files are private to the app and only accessible to you.
  • Optional Google Drive backup. Link Drive to back up PDFs to your own account. OAuth tokens are encrypted with Android Keystore-backed AES-256 and stored locally.
  • App settings on device. Language, default color mode, auto-crop preference, Pro license state — all stored in SharedPreferences on your device.

Privacy

PDFs, settings, and OAuth tokens are stored locally. None of this is uploaded unless you explicitly link Drive and trigger a backup. We have no server. We do not see, collect, or transmit your documents.
