About Scan Tools

Scan Tools puts the everyday document jobs in one place: clean document scans, high-resolution photo capture, signatures, text extraction, QR scanning and PDF conversion, with your files kept together.

What it does

  • Document scanner that produces clean, easy-to-read digital copies
  • High-resolution photo capture for documents, notes and receipts
  • Sign documents and images digitally
  • Extract printed text from images with OCR
  • Scan QR codes and open the information they contain
  • Convert images into PDF files, and browse saved PDFs and photos in one library

Questions

Is this the app formerly called Scan2Drive?

Yes. The app is listed on Google Play as Scan Tools; the package name is unchanged (com.llcailabs.scan2drive), so existing installs update normally.

Can I request deletion of my data?

Yes — see the data deletion page for the supported request process.

What does OCR do here?

It extracts printed text from images so you can reuse notes, receipts and document text instead of retyping it.
