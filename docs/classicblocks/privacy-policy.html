# Privacy Policy – Classic Blocks **Last updated: November 14, 2026** LLCAI Labs ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Classic Blocks mobile application handles information. ## 1. Information We Collect Classic Blocks does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play the game. We do not collect, transmit, or maintain personal information on our own servers through the game. ## 2. Data Stored on Your Device Classic Blocks stores certain game information locally on your device, such as: - Your game high score. - Local game preferences or settings, if applicable. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the application or clearing its app data may remove locally stored information. ## 3. Internet Access and Third-Party Services The core gameplay of Classic Blocks works offline. The current version does not integrate advertising services, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or third-party account sign-in services. We do not share game data with third parties through the application. ## 4. Data Security Since game records are stored locally on your device, their security also depends on your device's operating system and security settings. We recommend keeping your device updated and protected. ## 5. Children's Privacy Classic Blocks does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Because the application does not require an account or collect personal information through its core gameplay, no personal information is intentionally gathered through normal game use. ## 6. Your Privacy Choices You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. Please note that this may permanently remove your saved high score and other local game information. ## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. ## 8.
