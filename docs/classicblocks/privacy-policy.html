Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information Classic Blocks handles, where game data is stored, and how to contact LLCAI Labs with privacy questions.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Classic Blocks does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play the game. LLCAI Labs does not collect personal information through the current version of the application.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The game stores certain information locally on your device to provide its gameplay features. This may include:

  • Your game high score.
  • Local game preferences or settings, where applicable.

This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to LLCAI Labs. Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored game information.

3. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The core gameplay of Classic Blocks works offline. The current version does not integrate advertising services, analytics services, crash-reporting services, or third-party account sign-in services.

LLCAI Labs does not share game data with third parties through the current version of the application.

4. Data Security

Game information is stored locally on your device. Its security also depends on your device's operating system, access controls, and security settings. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date.

5. Children's Privacy

Classic Blocks does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The current version does not require an account or ask players to submit personal information to use its core gameplay features.

6. Your Privacy Choices

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application. Please be aware that doing so may also delete your saved high score and other local game information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Classic Blocks' privacy practices, contact:
