1. Introduction

LLC AI LABS ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the FreeTalk mobile application ("FreeTalk" or the "App") accesses, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the App.

App Name: FreeTalk

Package Name: com.llcailabs.freetalk

Developer: LLC AI LABS

Privacy Contact: llcailabs@gmail.com

FreeTalk is a push-to-talk (PTT) voice communication application that allows users to communicate with friends through direct voice communication and channels.

2. Information We Access and Process

FreeTalk accesses and processes certain information on your device and exchanges necessary information with connected devices to provide its communication features.

2.1 Microphone and Voice Data

FreeTalk uses microphone access when you activate the push-to-talk feature.

  • Audio is captured when you activate push-to-talk to transmit your voice.
  • Voice data is transmitted to the intended connected device or communication channel.
  • Received voice data is processed and played through your device's audio output.
  • We do not operate a cloud voice-recording service or store voice communications on our own servers.

2.2 Background Voice Playback

FreeTalk supports background voice playback so that users can continue hearing incoming voice transmissions while the App is running in the background.

  • Incoming audio may continue playing when the App is not visible on the screen.
  • Audio playback may continue while the device screen is off, subject to Android system and device settings.
  • Background playback is used to provide continuous voice communication.
  • Background playback does not mean that the microphone is continuously recording.

2.3 Device and Friend Information

To support device discovery, friend connections, and authentication, FreeTalk may process information such as:

  • A locally generated device identifier.
  • Device identity and authentication information.
  • Friend identifiers and friend-related information.
  • Connection addresses and connection status.
  • Information exchanged between devices to establish and maintain a connection.

2.4 Network Information

FreeTalk uses network capabilities to discover compatible nearby devices and establish direct connections.

Network information, such as local network addresses and connection details, may be processed to support communication between devices.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or processed by FreeTalk is used to:

  • Enable push-to-talk voice communication.
  • Support channel-based voice communication.
  • Receive and play incoming voice transmissions.
  • Support background voice playback.
  • Discover nearby compatible devices.
  • Identify and authenticate friends.
  • Establish and manage device connections.
  • Display connection and presence status.
  • Maintain the App's essential functionality.

We do not use this information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or marketing analytics.

4. Data Sharing

FreeTalk does not sell or rent user information to advertisers or data brokers.

FreeTalk does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting services.

Voice data and necessary connection information may be transmitted directly between participating devices when communication features are used.

We do not operate a cloud server to collect or store users' voice communications or friend information.

Information transmitted to another device may be accessible to the recipient of that communication.

5. Data Storage and Retention

FreeTalk is designed to process communication and friend information locally on the user's device.

Certain information, such as device identity, friend information, and connection-related data, may remain stored locally to support the App's functionality.

Received audio is processed for playback. Background playback itself does not mean that audio is uploaded to our servers.

If an optional voice history or recording feature is available in the installed version, audio files may be stored locally on the device. Such files are not uploaded to our servers.

We do not maintain a cloud database of user accounts, friend lists, or voice recordings.

6. Data Security

FreeTalk uses device identity and authentication mechanisms to help ensure that connections are established with intended communication partners.

Information stored locally is subject to the security protections provided by the Android operating system and the user's device.

No method of electronic communication or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over a local network or stored on a device.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use and your Android version, FreeTalk may request permissions such as:

  • Microphone: To capture your voice when you use push-to-talk.
  • Nearby Devices / Network: To discover nearby compatible devices and establish communication connections.
  • Background Execution / Foreground Service: To support continued voice communication and background audio playback when permitted by Android.

You can manage applicable permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling required permissions may prevent certain App features from working.

8. Children's Privacy

FreeTalk is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. Users should use the App under appropriate parental guidance where required by applicable law.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can control the App's access to device permissions through Android settings.

You may remove locally stored App information by using the applicable removal features within the App or by clearing the App's local data or uninstalling the App.

Deleting local data or uninstalling the App may remove locally stored friend information, device identity information, and locally stored audio files.

Information already received by another communication participant is outside our control.

Any updated version will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date.
